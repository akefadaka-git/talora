## 1. Purpose

This register is the evidence record for the planned 30-fixture Amharic/Ethiopic acceptance corpus. It does not itself grant rights, establish authorship, or certify any fixture as cleared.

The corpus must use original, commissioned, or otherwise explicitly authorized material. Public availability, an online download, a test self-roundtrip, or an author's name without a license/permission record is not proof of the rights TALORA needs.

## 2. Required permission scope

For each fixture, record the rights-holder's permission or license for every intended use:
- repository storage and version control;
- automated CI execution, parsing, rendering and regression testing;
- commercial product QA and internal evaluation;
- modification, normalization, conversion and creation of derived test outputs;
- redistribution or publication, only if the fixture is intended to be distributed with TALORA or otherwise made public;
- attribution wording, copyright notice, license text and any share-alike or other conditions;
- retention period, withdrawal terms and contact/permission evidence location.

Do not assume that a license permitting reading or personal use also permits commercial QA, repository storage, modification or redistribution.

## 3. Preferred acquisition route

1. Create original synthetic screenplay/stage-play material specifically for TALORA, with no copied dialogue, plot, characters or protected expression from existing works.
2. Record the human author(s) and rights-holder for each item.
3. Obtain a written grant or license that explicitly covers the uses listed above. If the material is generated or co-authored using an AI tool, have the responsible rights-holder confirm the intended rights and permitted uses; do not rely on an assumption about copyrightability.
4. Assign a stable fixture ID and compute a SHA-256 hash of the exact file.
5. Record format, language, complexity/coverage tags, license/permission reference, required notices and reviewer notes.
6. Only after rights clearance, place the fixture in the approved corpus and use it in repository/CI/commercial QA.
7. Preserve the source text, approved fixture file, rights evidence and hash together in the controlled evidence record.

Third-party published scripts or plays may be used only with specific written permission or a license that clearly authorizes the full intended use. No rights are inferred.

## 4. Register fields

Every row must be completed before a fixture is marked **CLEARED**:
- Fixture ID and filename;
- format and language;
- author and rights-holder;
- provenance/source;
- exact license name/version or written permission reference/date;
- repository/CI/commercial-QA/derivative/redistribution permissions, each separately marked yes/no/conditional;
- attribution and NOTICE text;
- restrictions, expiry, withdrawal and retention conditions;
- SHA-256 of the approved fixture file;
- status and evidence reviewer/date.

## 6. Acceptance corpus coverage

The 30 items must collectively exercise the approved acceptance surface, not merely repeat short greeting strings. The coverage plan should include:
- screenplay and stage-play structures, scene headings, dialogue, character cues and stage directions;
- Ethiopic punctuation, numerals, word boundaries, mixed Ethiopic/Latin text and line wrapping;
- combining marks, normalization-sensitive sequences, code-point preservation and shaping;
- long and short dialogue blocks, page/scene breaks, headings and metadata;
- import/export and round-trip cases for each format that remains in the v1 supported-format promise;
- font fallback, missing-glyph detection, PDF embedding and rendering stress cases;
- malformed/edge-case inputs for safe rejection and recovery.

The fixture distribution across formats/languages must be specified before acceptance execution. This register does not silently expand the supported-format promise; FDX remains excluded from v1 under ODR-13 unless the Owner explicitly amends that decision.
