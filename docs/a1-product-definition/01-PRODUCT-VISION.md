# Product Vision and Scope

## Mission

TALORA combines professional screenplay and stage-play authoring with story development, visual planning and production workflows in one connected workspace, with IROKA intelligence integrated across the product.

## Vision

Make professional creative-production tools accessible to Ethiopian creators and build an internationally capable platform originating in Ethiopia.

## Target users

### Individual creators
- Screenwriters, playwrights and script editors.
- Film, television, documentary and animation writers.
- Theatre, musical, opera and radio-drama writers.
- Directors, independent filmmakers and stage directors.
- Students, educators and emerging creators.

### Organizations
- Film and television production companies.
- Theatre and performing-arts companies.
- Film schools and universities.
- Independent production teams and creative agencies.

## Product pillars

1. **Write:** Structured screenplay and stage-play authoring.
2. **Develop:** Ideas, loglines, treatments, outlines, characters and scenes.
3. **Visualize:** Storyboards, shot plans and stage blocking.
4. **Prepare:** Script breakdown, cast, locations, props, costumes and schedules.
5. **Collaborate:** Review, comments, permissions, revisions and shared workspaces.
6. **Intelligence:** Context-aware IROKA assistance with authorial control.
7. **Publish and export:** Reliable professional documents and portable project data.

## Scope

The initial client is Android. The architecture should allow future desktop and web clients to reuse shared document models, APIs and production services.

The first release must be a useful professional writing product even when IROKA cloud services are unavailable. AI is an integrated capability, not a prerequisite for basic writing.

## Product principles

- Professional tools before novelty features.
- Structured documents rather than plain-text-only scripts.
- Offline-first operation.
- Multilingual and Ethiopic-script correctness.
- User ownership and portability.
- Transparent AI suggestions and explicit approval.
- Accessible free tier without destructive restrictions.
- Sustainable, clearly disclosed monetization.
- Modular architecture and testable interfaces.

## Non-goals for initial release

- Building a full non-linear video editor.
- Replacing professional camera, sound or lighting hardware systems.
- Guaranteeing commercial success or production budgets.
- Automatically making creative decisions on behalf of authors.
- Implementing cryptocurrency or unrelated IROKA capabilities.
- Launching desktop/web clients before Android foundation is validated.
