## 1. Baseline identity

TALORA is an Ethiopia-first, Android-first professional creative-writing and production platform for screenwriters, playwrights, filmmakers, theatre professionals, independent creators and production organizations. It is designed for international extensibility.

**Positioning:** Imagine. Write. Direct. Produce.  
**Technology attribution:** Powered by IROKA.

The name TALORA is Owner-approved as the proposed application name, subject to trademark, company-name, domain and distribution-store clearance.

## 4. Stage gates

- **A1:** Product assessment, requirements, architecture and specification.
- **A2:** Read-only technical reconciliation, IROKA compatibility, dependency and security assessment, and implementation planning.
- **A3:** Implementation only after explicit Owner authorization.
- **A4:** Verification and remediation with evidence.
- **A5:** Deployment and publication under separate authorization.

## 5. Governing principles

1. Owner-governed, explicit stage approvals.
2. Preserve unrelated repository work.
3. No silent changes to a writer's original creative content.
4. Offline-first usefulness and reliable synchronization.
5. Ethiopic Unicode and multilingual localization.
6. Privacy and ownership of unpublished creative works.
7. Open, documented interfaces and exportable user data.
8. No unsupported claim of production readiness or legal clearance.

## 6. A1 acceptance

A1 may be closed only after the Owner reviews and approves the complete requirements, architecture, monetization, privacy, localization, roadmap, acceptance criteria and open-decision register. This commit set is a review candidate, not an A1 closure record.
