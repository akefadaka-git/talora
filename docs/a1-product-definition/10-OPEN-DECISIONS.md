## Assumptions to validate

- Android devices used by target creators can support the proposed editing experience.
- Suitable Ethiopic fonts and rendering behavior can be licensed and shipped.
- IROKA can expose supported, permission-aware capabilities through documented interfaces.
- Affordable hosting and synchronization can be operated sustainably.
- Advertising and payment providers can legally and technically serve the target market.
- Professional import/export formats can be implemented with acceptable compatibility.

## A2 investigation questions

1. What IROKA repositories, services, protocols and licenses are available for integration?
2. Which existing IROKA components can be reused without changing their governance?
3. What are the Android minimum SDK and device-support requirements?
4. Which document formats and compatibility fixtures can be legally used for testing?
5. What are the data residency, encryption, backup and recovery requirements?
6. Which Ethiopian payment and advertising providers are eligible and operational?
7. What are realistic cloud, AI inference and storage costs?
8. What legal and store-policy reviews are required before launch?

No unresolved item should be silently treated as approved or implemented.
