# Monetization and Entitlements

## Commercial model

TALORA targets individuals and organizations through a free advertising-supported edition and paid ad-free premium access.

## Free edition

Proposed capabilities:
- Core screenplay and stage-play editors.
- Project management and basic templates.
- Basic character and scene management.
- Offline writing.
- Standard PDF export.
- Basic production features.
- Limited IROKA assistance.
- Advertisements in designated non-disruptive surfaces.

Free users must retain useful ownership, editing and export capabilities. Advertising must not interrupt typing, dialogue entry, editing or script preview.

## Premium edition

Proposed model: one-time purchase for a perpetual license to the defined premium core software features.

Proposed capabilities:
- All Free features.
- No advertisements.
- Advanced formatting and revisions.
- Advanced story, character and continuity tools.
- Professional import/export formats.
- Advanced production and storyboard capabilities.
- Expanded offline functionality.
- Defined premium IROKA usage allowance.

A lifetime core software license must not be represented as unlimited lifetime cloud inference. Cloud AI, storage and collaboration have continuing operating costs. The exact included allowance, fair-use limits and optional additional usage purchases must be disclosed before purchase and must not silently convert the core license into a subscription.

## Organizational licensing

A separate organization offering may include team workspaces, access roles, centralized project administration, review/approval workflows, shared production planning and organization-level billing. Pricing and seat model remain open.

## Advertising safeguards

- No advertisements inside exported creative documents.
- No ads embedded in script content.
- No sale of unpublished scripts or private creative content.
- No ad targeting derived from private screenplay or stage-play text.
- Clearly distinguish advertising from product recommendations.
- Provide an ad-free upgrade path.
- Minimize collection and sharing of advertising identifiers.

## Entitlements and billing architecture

Entitlements must be verified server-side where applicable and cached securely for offline use with defined expiry and recovery rules. Support purchase restoration, refunds/revocations, account migration and device changes.

Do not hardcode prices, payment provider assumptions or entitlement states in the client.

## External dependencies requiring validation

- Google Play distribution and billing eligibility.
- Ethiopian payment gateway availability and integration terms.
- Advertising network eligibility and publisher payout support in Ethiopia.
- Tax, consumer protection and foreign-exchange requirements.
- Refund and customer support obligations.
- App-store policy compliance.

No provider is selected or activated at A1.

## Pricing research

Set Ethiopian ETB and international prices only after competitor, affordability, willingness-to-pay, payment access and operating-cost research. Do not invent a price or promise a payment method before validation.
