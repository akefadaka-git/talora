# Product Requirements

Priority notation: **P0** release foundation; **P1** important subsequent capability; **P2** later enhancement. Priorities are proposed for A1 review.

## Project and workspace management
- P0: Create, rename, duplicate, archive and organize projects.
- P0: Store project type, genre, language, owner and status.
- P1: Workspaces for teams and organizations.
- P1: Project templates and reusable assets.

## Screenplay Studio
- P0: Feature, short-film and episodic script documents.
- P0: Scene headings, action, character cues, dialogue, parentheticals and transitions.
- P0: Keyboard-aware editing, undo/redo, autosave and scene navigation.
- P0: Automatic screenplay formatting and print preview.
- P1: Dual dialogue, scene numbering, revision colors and locked revisions.
- P1: Script comparison and production draft management.

## Stage Writing Studio
- P0: Acts, scenes, characters, dialogue and stage directions.
- P0: Entrances, exits and scene transitions.
- P1: Blocking notes, cue references and rehearsal drafts.
- P1: Musicals, librettos, radio drama and specialized templates.
- P2: Advanced prompt-book and performance workflows.

## Story development and characters
- P1: Ideas, loglines, synopsis, treatments, outlines and beat sheets.
- P1: Character biographies, objectives, motivations, relationships and arcs.
- P1: Scene and dialogue analysis.
- P1: Narrative structure and continuity tracking.

## Document engine and formatting
- P0: Structured, typed document elements independent of UI presentation.
- P0: Stable document identity and revision history.
- P0: Deterministic pagination and export validation.
- P1: Multiple professional templates and configurable style profiles.
- P1: Import conversion reports and recoverable conversion errors.

## Storyboard and visualization
- P1: Panels, scene references, shot descriptions and image references.
- P1: Reorderable sequences and storyboard export.
- P2: Advanced camera and visual-planning workflows.

## Production breakdown and management
- P1: Extract and review characters, cast, locations, props, costumes, equipment and effects.
- P1: Shooting and rehearsal schedules, stripboards and production calendars.
- P1: Call sheets and production reports.
- P2: Crew assignment, availability, budget estimates and advanced scheduling.

## Stage management
- P1: Prompt book, lighting and sound cues, actor entrances/exits, props and blocking records.
- P1: Rehearsal notes and performance versions.

## Collaboration
- P1: Shared projects, invitations and role-based permissions.
- P1: Comments, annotations, review and approval.
- P1: Change tracking and conflict-aware synchronization.
- P2: Real-time co-editing after consistency and recovery design is validated.

## Media and search
- P0: Project attachments and local media organization.
- P1: Images, audio, video and research references.
- P1: Search across projects, documents, scenes, dialogue, characters and production assets.

## Import and export
- P0: PDF and plain-text export.
- P1: Fountain and DOCX import/export.
- P1: Final Draft FDX import/export, subject to format compatibility tests.
- P1: Production breakdown, storyboard, rehearsal and schedule reports.
- P0: Export must preserve source content and report unsupported elements.
- P1: Portable project archive and user data export.

## IROKA intelligence
- P1: Story Architect, Script Doctor, Character Intelligence and Continuity Guardian.
- P1: Production Intelligence, Cinematic Intelligence and Stage Director Intelligence.
- P1: Translation, localization and contextual research assistance.
- P0: AI suggestions are distinguishable from author content.
- P0: No silent edits; material changes require explicit approval.
- P0: Preserve a record of accepted AI-generated changes.

## Account, entitlements and administration
- P0: Secure identity, project ownership and access control.
- P1: Free and premium entitlement handling.
- P1: Purchase restoration and license verification.
- P2: Organization administration and licensing.

## Quality attributes
- Responsive editing and crash recovery.
- Offline-first project creation and editing.
- Accessible controls and scalable tablet layouts.
- Battery and memory efficiency.
- Secure transport and protected local data.
- Testable synchronization and export determinism.
- Localization without assumptions that all scripts use Latin characters.
